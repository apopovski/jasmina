YOUR FREE GUIDE

7 days of good food.
One simple start.

A flexible, plant-forward anti-inflammatory meal plan with everyday ingredients. Mix, match, and adjust portions to your appetite and needs.

THE
EVERYDAY
TABLE✳7-DAY MEAL PLAN

Each day includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, and an optional snack. Prep a few basics ahead of time — cooked grains, washed greens, and a simple dressing — to make the week easier.

These are meal ideas, not a personalized treatment plan. If you have allergies, medical conditions, or specific nutrition needs, adapt them with your healthcare professional.

THE WEEK AHEAD

Your 7-day menu.

DAY 01

A fresh start

Breakfast
Overnight oats with berries, walnuts, and chia.
Lunch
Quinoa bowl with chickpeas, cucumber, greens, and lemon-tahini dressing.
Dinner
Baked salmon or tofu with roasted broccoli and sweet potato.
Snack
Apple with almond butter.
DAY 02

Keep it colorful

Breakfast
Plain yogurt or soy yogurt with berries, pumpkin seeds, and oats.
Lunch
Leftover salmon or tofu over greens with quinoa and avocado.
Dinner
Lentil and vegetable soup with whole-grain toast.
Snack
Carrots and hummus.
DAY 03

Easy does it

Breakfast
Whole-grain toast with avocado, egg or tofu scramble, and tomato.
Lunch
Leftover lentil soup with a side salad.
Dinner
Ginger-garlic tofu or chicken stir-fry with brown rice and mixed vegetables.
Snack
A pear and a handful of walnuts.
DAY 04

Midweek balance

Breakfast
Oatmeal with sliced banana, cinnamon, and ground flaxseed.
Lunch
Leftover stir-fry over greens or brown rice.
Dinner
White bean and spinach skillet with tomatoes, olive oil, and whole-grain bread.
Snack
Plain yogurt or soy yogurt with berries.
DAY 05

Simple favorites

Breakfast
Berry-spinach smoothie with unsweetened milk, oats, and nut butter.
Lunch
White bean wrap with greens, cucumber, and hummus.
Dinner
Sheet-pan chicken or chickpeas with peppers, zucchini, and quinoa.
Snack
Orange and pumpkin seeds.
DAY 06

Room to enjoy

Breakfast
Egg or tofu scramble with spinach, tomato, and whole-grain toast.
Lunch
Leftover sheet-pan vegetables and protein in a grain bowl.
Dinner
Whole-grain pasta with tomato sauce, mushrooms, spinach, and a side salad.
Snack
Apple and a handful of nuts.
DAY 07

Finish feeling good

Breakfast
Yogurt or soy yogurt bowl with banana, berries, and chia.
Lunch
Chickpea, avocado, and veggie salad with olive oil and lemon.
Dinner
Vegetable and bean chili with brown rice and fresh herbs.
Snack
Carrots with hummus.

TAKE THIS TO THE STORE

Your grocery list.

Check what you already have first. Choose the proteins and dairy alternatives that work for you.

Produce

Berries, bananas, apples, pears, orange, lemon, avocado, spinach, mixed greens, broccoli, sweet potatoes, carrots, cucumber, tomatoes, bell peppers, zucchini, mushrooms, garlic, ginger, fresh herbs.

Proteins & legumes

Chickpeas, lentils, white beans, kidney or black beans, hummus, tofu and/or salmon, chicken, eggs.

Grains & pantry

Rolled oats, quinoa, brown rice, whole-grain bread, wraps and pasta, canned tomatoes, olive oil, tahini, almond or other nut butter, cinnamon.

Extras

Plain yogurt or soy yogurt, unsweetened milk of choice, walnuts, pumpkin seeds, chia seeds, ground flaxseed.

Start where you are. One nourishing choice at a time.
