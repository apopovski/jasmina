WHAT I BELIEVE

It’s about adding more of what nourishes you.

I believe in inclusion rather than restriction. When you consistently add nourishing foods and supportive routines, unhealthy patterns begin to fade naturally. Health is not about perfection — it’s about building habits that align with your long-term wellbeing.

MY STORY

A personal journey turned a purpose.

My passion for nutrition began during my freshman year of college when I experienced firsthand how poor habits affected my energy and performance. Learning how daily food choices influence long-term health transformed my life and sparked a calling to help others experience the same empowerment.

Having lived, studied, and worked across Europe, the United States, and Australia, I bring a global appreciation for food culture and whole-food traditions into my practice.
